- Tell me about a time you were in a conflict with a co-worker and how you handled it?
	My final school project was a group project, and one of my teammates began missing classes and meetings without communicating with us. We were understanding as long as he continued completing his work, but as we approached the presentation and final report, he became unresponsive. He was responsible for the final report, and after a week of trying to contact him without a response, I reached out to our professor to explain the situation.

Before doing so, I told my teammate that we weren't upset with him and that all we needed was communication. I let him know that if he needed help, we were willing to take on additional tasks, and he didn't need to share any personal details—just let us know what was going on. After contacting the professor, he eventually reached out, and we were able to come together as a team and complete the final report that same day.

This experience taught me the importance of communication, accountability, and being understanding when working as a team.
